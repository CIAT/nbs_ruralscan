# v1.6.12 — catalogue #29 + #30 (retro of the 2026-10-19 effect round)

Extraction contract text unchanged; units under v1.6.0–v1.6.11 stay valid, no re-stamp.

## #29 — a spread is not a sign
A range of estimates across implementers/sites/methods is dispersion, not a direction.
Instance: ev_project_cost_veno12_1 (dam-rehabilitation cost spread by implementer encoded as
direction=positive on a cost variable). If the quote states only a spread: direction=none,
range to economic_value_range (gates permitting) or outcome_raw.

## #30 — an annualised establishment cost is not a recurrent cost
Route a cost by what the money buys, never by the denominator alone. Instance: Taillardat 2020
(wet_mitigation_potential_cost_effectiveness_2020) reports restoration cost annualised to
$ ha−1 y−1 for cost-effectiveness; it is establishment capital, not maintenance. Recurrent
requires the paper to name ongoing operations.
