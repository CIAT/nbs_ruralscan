## Why zero T4

The review explains windbreak operation, response to shelter and implementation design. It does not present a land-suitability model, establishment envelope, host-system eligibility test or hard exclusion. Its genuinely actionable general guidance concerns **design geometry and management**: length at least10×height, perpendicular orientation to problem winds, continuity/gaps, optical versus aerodynamic density, and snow-capture design. These belong in M6 design guidance; existing VONT does not provide those design variables. Do not relabel orientation as terrain aspect, optical density as landscape canopy cover, ordinary wind-speed effects as storm hazard, or protected-zone distances as distance to water.

Low water-holding capacity and limited soil moisture passages describe **crop response/tradeoffs** in sheltered areas, not where shelterbelt trees can establish. Temperature, rain, snow and frost passages likewise describe microclimate effects. Source gives no calibrated soil/rainfall/temperature thresholds. No direction-only T4 vote is justified merely by variable mention.
