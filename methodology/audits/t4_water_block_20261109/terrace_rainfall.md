The methods (PDF p. 5) describe constructing the spatial input from the relation between elevation and seasonal rainfall. Figure 3 (PDF p. 6) identifies the input as mean October–April rainfall. **WOCAT (2013).** PDF p. 129 describes forward-sloping bench terraces in areas receiving 200–600 mm/year, mainly for trees and bushes. Runoff comes from the uncropped areas between terraces, with catchment-to-application area ratios of 1:1–10:1 and slopes up to 60% grade. Retain the annual use range for that design. The text supplies no suitability score or measured failure threshold outside the range.

**Critchley & Siegert (1991).** The FAO manual, §§5.7.1–2, describes 200–750 mm rainfall in arid to semi-arid areas for contour stone lines harvesting water for crops. It distinguishes these structures from hillside terracing. Keep the original stone-line profile, including the preference for slopes below 2%, outside terrace scoring and support.
