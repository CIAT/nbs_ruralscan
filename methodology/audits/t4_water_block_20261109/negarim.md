# Negarim tree microcatchments

Pete approved retaining four rainfall and slope profiles on 9 November 2026. The two mappings preserve source guidance for siting Negarim tree microcatchments. Neither 150 mm/year of rainfall nor 5% slope triggers an exclusion. Suitability outside the reported conditions remains unscored.

| Source and locator | Evidence role | Conditions retained |
|---|---|---|
| Critchley & Siegert (FAO, 1991), §5.2.2 | Siting guidance | Annual rainfall can be as low as 150 mm. Recommended slopes range from flat to 5% grade. Subdivide the block where terrain is uneven. |
| Same manual, §5.2.1 | Negev example | The manual describes Negarim systems mostly on research farms in an area where rainfall is as low as 100–150 mm/year. The figure describes the area's climate. |
| WOCAT (2013), PDF p. 125, printed p. 112, Negarim paragraph | Common use | Common on 1–5% grades in areas with 150–500 mm/year. Keep the rainfall and slope conditions paired. |
| Same paragraph | Broader reported use | Negarim have been applied on 1–20% grades. WOCAT gives no corresponding rainfall interval for the full slope range. |

For example, an 8% grade at 300 mm/year falls within WOCAT’s broader reported slope range and outside both the common slope range and FAO’s recommended slope range. The sources give no suitability score for this combination. WOCAT credits a design illustration to Critchley & Siegert. Independence of the numerical ranges remains unresolved.
