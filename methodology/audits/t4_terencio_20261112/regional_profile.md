# Terêncio’s ex-field water-harvesting planning profile

Pete approved four linked cards from Terêncio et al. (2017) as one regional model in Portugal’s Ave River basin. The study mapped regional suitability and compared six candidate sub-catchments for a 400 ha irrigation project. Table 1 (PDF p. 5) gives the following class ranks on a 1–5 scale. Table 2 (p. 7) describes the mapped inputs. Figure 4 (p. 8) shows their distributions.

The generic planning stage weights every factor and group at 1. The irrigation example gives the physical, socioeconomic and ecological groups weights 3, 5 and 1, respectively. Individual weights for these four cards remain 1 in both stages. The three physical cards and the ecological slope card retain their separate group memberships. The study includes engineered installations. Shared T4 scores, unmatched inputs and ambiguous boundaries remain open. Existing binding and calibration holds still apply.
