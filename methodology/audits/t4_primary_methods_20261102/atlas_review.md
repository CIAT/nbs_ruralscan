# Global forest-restoration atlas: method referral, not family eligibility

Two companion publications, one mapping lineage. All seven native pages read; Laestadius pages 1–2 and brochure pages 2–5 rendered and inspected, including maps, legends and charts. Brochure page 1 is the title/cover. No source-level independent-study count is inferred.

Sources: Laestadius et al., *Mapping opportunities for forest landscape restoration*, FAO Unasylva 238, pp47–48, https://www.fao.org/4/i2560e/i2560e08.pdf; Minnemeyer et al., *A World of Opportunity*, https://pdf.wri.org/world_of_opportunity_brochure_2011-09.pdf. Laestadius visible issue is 2011/2, while the IUFRO bibliography/search metadata says 2012: unresolved citation-year discrepancy. Hidden PDF text includes an obsolete 2003 running header; it is not the visible issue date. Neither publication was promoted to SRC.
