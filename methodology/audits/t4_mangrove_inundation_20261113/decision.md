# Mangrove establishment and time flooded

Pete retained the Global Mangrove Alliance’s 50% cutoff as generalizable establishment evidence on 13 November 2026. Section 3.4.4 (PDF p. 39, printed p. 76) describes difficulty establishing **most species** when the site is tidally flooded for **more than half the time**. The evidence register retains the original quote, numerical bound, practice scope and Namita’s approval. The reviewed interpretation records the strict comparator, unit, outcome and population.

| Example site | Use in screening |
|---|---|
| 49% or exactly 50% of time flooded | The source’s warning does not trigger. Suitability remains undetermined. |
| 55% of time flooded | Flag establishment difficulty. Inspect the intended species and inundation at local reference sites. The family score remains undetermined. |
| Flooded on 55% of days, duration unknown | The percentage of total time flooded is unknown. |

The 2023 guideline also describes low-intertidal planting failures on PDF p. 109. These two passages come from one publication. Lewis (2005, PDF pp. 7, 9–10) calls for comparing local flood depth, duration and frequency. Its observations from existing forests include a basin forest inundated 59.4% of the time. Van Loon et al. (2016, Table 2, PDF p. 4) uses minutes per day and minutes per inundation together for species groups. The five species cards retain their existing review status. Neither study supplies a family-wide establishment score at 50%.

Before this decision the accepted mangrove CSV contained zero mappings, while replay emitted a trapezoidal response with only an absolute maximum of 50. The evidence-specific numeric compilation policy now preserves the warning as reference evidence and holds conversion to a shared scoring function. With the accepted inputs, isolated and live compilations agree. A scored response would require evidence connecting establishment to the intended species, local tidal regime and site condition.
