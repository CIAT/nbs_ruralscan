## Proposed evidence and limits

One unapproved `scoping_candidate`: `ev_forest_regeneration_potential_fao175_ch4_20261104`, `regeneration_potential`, `forest_restoration__assisted_regeneration`, structural suitability. Source PDF72 explicitly conditions natural-regeneration advantage on mature healthy seed trees in or near the restoration area. The conditional strategy guidance is retained; it does not prove regeneration impossible without seed trees, and the next sentence explicitly allows stump shoots to support restoration; no scored gate, exclusion mask, monotonic function, calibrated response or runtime threshold is created.

The same source gives **species-dependent 50–100 m** from the nearest seed trees. This is retained only as a reported source value and explicit proxy/transfer hold. An individual healthy seed tree is not necessarily the edge of the nearest forest. No `distance_to_forest` unit is emitted. No new VONT, family or numerical assumption is introduced. Stump shoots are supporting text, not a reason to copy agroforestry `rootstock_presence` into forest restoration.

Zero other units are proposed. Most remaining material addresses how to restore, species selection, nursery practice, management, prioritisation, other NbS, or effects. The low-degradation strategy passage is retained as a candidate hold: protection, ANR and enrichment are mixed; no class scale or single-family rule is established. Planting timing based on sufficient soil water is not a spatial soil-moisture or annual-rainfall envelope.

## Critical retained holds

- **FMNR PDF73:** explicitly farmland; rootstock/seedbank and tree/property rights remain agroforestry/M6 pointers. Not relabelled forest restoration.
- **Species:** Acacia albida farming-system effect (PDF68); huarango common-name case (PDF74); Prosopis juliflora invasive effect (PDF75); Populus simonii provenance/afforestation case and Adansonia digitata/Faidherbia albida/Prosopis africana parkland domestication (PDF76). No named-species envelope or taxon is invented; these remain separate scoped holds, not discarded evidence.
- **Species-selection method:** soil risks, hydrological balance, biodiversity, markets and cultural preference (PDF73–77) are not practice-level establishment curves. Nursery proximity to water/site (PDF77) does not become landscape road/water distance.
- **Operational and asset risks:** participation, tenure, governance, costs, fire/grazing management, planting material, weeds, termites and browsing remain M2b/M6 holds. Fire/grazing can benefit some ecosystems; no blanket direction or exclusion is inferred.
- **Design numbers:** 60×60×40 cm planting-hole example, 250 mm rainfall → 250 plants/ha dune-fixation density example, first-two-year watering, and 3–20-year ANR duration remain design/context values, not suitability thresholds.
- **Other NbS:** Box4.4 microcatchments 10–500 m²; macrocatchments 2–200 ha; terrace slopes 4–50%, short slopes <4%, long slopes >0.5% remain water-harvesting/soil-conservation pointers with cited-primary attribution. Never forest-restoration thresholds. Rooftop/courtyard harvesting is out of landscape scope. Fog devices/plastic Waterboxx and wastewater irrigation are not automatically accepted as NbS.
- **Plantation boundary:** chapter mixes restoration and production forestry. Commercial planting/provenance claims are not automatically evidence for restoration toward a natural baseline; the chapter explicitly warns against converting diverse natural forest to monotypic/reduced-species plantations (PDF77).
