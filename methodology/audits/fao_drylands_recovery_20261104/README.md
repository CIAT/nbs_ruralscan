One unapproved structural scoping candidate was admitted: `ev_forest_regeneration_potential_fao175_ch4_20261104`, for `regeneration_potential` in `forest_restoration__assisted_regeneration`. The same quote retains stump shoots as another route. This does not establish that regeneration is impossible without nearby seed trees.

It remains a reported-value hold, not a `distance_to_forest` threshold, scored gate, exclusion mask, direction curve or runtime weight. The secondary Heidelberg et al. (2011) attribution is preserved in `lineage_of`; its primary publication has not been acquired or reviewed in this pass.
