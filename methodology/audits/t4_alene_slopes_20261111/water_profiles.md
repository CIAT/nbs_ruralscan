An 800 mm modelled depth is S2. A Curve Number of 80 alone supplies insufficient information to assign a rank. The calculation used rainfall from nine stations. The datasets section gives 2008–2017 and the runoff method gives 2008–2018. Compare future inputs on quantity, temporal basis and spatial support. A density of 1.75 km/km² ranks S3 within the study network. Applying it elsewhere requires a comparable definition of streams, network resolution and contributing area. Neither ranking resolves assignment at shared internal endpoints. Missing inputs receive no score.
