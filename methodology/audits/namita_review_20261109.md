# Namita’s 9 November review retrospective

The audit covered all 40 distinct evidence × reviewer decisions in [PR #655](https://github.com/CIAT/nbs_ruralscan/pull/655). There were 25 structural-suitability and 15 operational-risk records, with 17 keeps, 22 drops and one query. The drop codes were insufficient context (7), unusable value (6), off scope (6), relationship missed (2) and wrong practice (1). Most reviews supply a reason code without individual explanatory prose. The lessons below are the retrospective’s interpretation of those codes alongside the evidence, including the positive examples. They do not attribute unstated reasoning to Namita.

The [decision ledger](../../pipeline/metrics/learnings_log.feedback.scoped.csv) gives the source evidence ID, decision, reviewer, feedback fingerprint and lesson for each card. Its baseline is the last complete feedback snapshot. Thirty-eight decisions differed from that snapshot. The Batcheler keep and Lohbeck query repeated the recorded feedback. Replaying the bounded ledger reduces the outstanding count from 213 to 175 at input revision 1773e86e. The global cursor remains at 2,311. Future changes to a verdict, reason or note re-enter the backlog. This was a review retrospective; no extraction sweep ran.

| Examples | What future extraction and review should do |
|---|---|
| Lohbeck FMNR rainfall; Batcheler site climate | Record the climate of observed implementation. Ask what the study establishes for sites beyond the observed range. Lohbeck’s 100–950 mm/year gives no evidence for excluding a site at 951 mm/year. The existing corrected card already captures this. Ruleset v1.10.0 adds an advisory check that catches the earlier encoding and accepts the corrected one. |
| Haile parkland carbon; Ranchi soil properties and rainfall | Recover every page of a ranked table, identify the measurement and its unit, and keep the weight column separate. A Keep can retain source ranks while transfer or calibration remains open. The later [Haile decision](t4_haile_parkland_20261109/decision.md) retains both pages as one unscored profile. The individual nitrogen drop supplies no grounds to reject other soil variables. |
| Chuma, Palma, Brandt, Unger, Kizir and Jawarneh | Existing T4 source-purpose, class-list and variable-identity checks apply. A predictor list, baseline land use, site climate mean, hypothetical legal scenario or livestock-targeted map needs separate evidence before it can supply a practice-siting rule. The operational route applies to supported implementation factors. |
| Pattanayak, Garbach, Bayard and Shrestha | Explain who or what the denominator counts. The proportion of studies including market distance or reporting significance cannot become an adoption probability or effect size. State the predictor’s sign, intervention, comparison and outcome. Management of an established alley and adoption of a new one answer different questions. Preserve regional, indirect and respondent-specific evidence at the level supported by each review. |
| Mushtaq, FAO and Lasco | Existing lineage rules apply. Retain the cited original and distinguish direct evidence from secondary reporting when comparing or counting studies. |
