## Mangrove follow-up after PR #665

Namita kept seven cards and dropped two. Four keeps concern implementation conditions, including guard-station capacity in Vietnam and local knowledge and participation in Sri Lanka. Three keeps concern spatial suitability. Lewis (PDF p. 7) uses nearby natural mangroves as a reference for depth, duration and frequency of tidal flooding. FFEM (p. 25) specifies the upper 20–30 cm as the sampling zone for sediment texture. Worthington and Spalding (p. 10) excluded historical mangrove losses eroded to permanent subtidal open water. Their 16% records the share of loss excluded. [Merged decisions](https://github.com/CIAT/nbs_ruralscan/pull/665)

The two drops illustrate different extraction errors. Villarreal (p. 8, off scope) overlaid community dynamics after ROOT selected priority areas. The layer therefore cannot explain site selection in that optimisation. The MAR manual (p. 44, relationship missed) describes nearby conserved mangroves supplying propagules for natural regeneration. The card encoded the direction as unclear despite recording the mechanism in its notes. Future readers should identify what proximity supports, which restoration pathway benefits and whether the source supplies a distance. These are interpretations of the recorded quotations and reason codes. Both drop decisions stand.

For the final recipe, the subtidal exclusion needs evidence of past mangrove cover and subsequent erosion to permanently submerged open water. Seasonal or intertidal inundation is compatible with mangrove habitat. At revision 4a2d7e7f, the ontology lists ESA WorldCover 2021 as a candidate for land cover. T1 also catalogues JRC surface-water occurrence and seasonality. The binding register contains no land-cover binding, and neither catalogue entry establishes that a formerly mangrove-covered site eroded below the intertidal zone. A broad water mask could therefore exclude viable habitat. An executable exclusion needs a validated layer or combination that reproduces the source distinction.
