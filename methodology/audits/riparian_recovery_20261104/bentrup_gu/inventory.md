# Bentrup and Gu: bounded recovery inventory

Ruleset v1.9.4. Authorised T4 recovery only. All PDF pages read in native text; all layout-dependent pages rendered and visually inspected. Exact reusable anchors are in `native_anchors.json`; complete native text and page renders remain in local gitignored staging; the original shared PDFs are the reproducible source. No human approval is claimed. Two admitted unapproved evidence units, both Bentrup, are listed below and in the Evidence Register. No quantitative response function is proposed.

## Scope and source identity

- Bentrup and Kellerman, *Where should buffers go? Modeling riparian habitat connectivity in northeast Kansas*: seven PDF pages, journal pages 209–215. Opening title visually verified; native author OCR says “Beatrug” but image clearly reads Bentrup. Registered identity checks remain central. Page 1 explicitly defines riparian buffers as plantings. Family `riparian_buffer__planted` is justified for this intervention, including planting to close existing gaps.
- Gu and Liu, *Applications of Remote Sensing and GIS to the Assessment of Riparian Zones for Environmental Restoration in Agricultural Watersheds*: seven PDF pages, publisher cover + journal pages 263–268. Source title and DOI visible on cover and opening article page. The source specifies long-term vegetation cover, agricultural land and restoration, but not planting, natural regeneration, or re-establishing woody vegetation. Neither FAM planted nor natural_restored assignment is established. All candidate Gu claims stay in an explicit **family hold**; “restoration” alone cannot establish the woody-reforestation family. Queue tags are not a scope decision.
- No supplements or supplementary-data references found in either complete paper. References are literature attribution, not independently reviewed primary sources. Neither source provides practice-level establishment response calibration.

## Verification and adversarial omission check

- Deterministic retrieval packages existed and were checked by source identity before emission. Full-page native text, not retrieval hit counts, defines coverage.
- The local staging builder slices contiguous source strings from `page.get_text()`, calls `validate_units`, verifies both primary and justification anchors in native pages, then uses `save_units`. No retyping of quoted text or cross-column stitching.
- Two variable-use records from one source, zero asserted suitability directions, zero calibrated response functions, zero human approvals. Two rows do not mean two independent studies. They share one geometry passage but describe distinct measured model inputs.
- `context.reported_values` preserves local total-width values; `relationship` is null. Source-specific width choices are not global parameters. Direction does not follow from increasing width by stream order.
- Bentrup source assumptions with no semantically matching VONT remain inventory holds. Existing remnant patch area cannot be renamed as restoration site area, connectivity distance as unbuffered-length ratio, or width inventory as existing vegetation width.
- Gu has zero emitted units because source does not establish an authorised family. Anchored retained claims make later review a lookup, not rediscovery. M4/secondary/future-work claims are additionally held from structural suitability.
- Native PDF text contains Bentrup OCR defects and interleaving, despite no OCR-needed pages in index. Visual reading resolved meaning, but does not satisfy text-only source guardrail for affected claims. Nine page renders inspected: Bentrup 1–6; Gu 4–6. All tables/figures included.
- Central intake, quote/context/provenance checks and retrospective ledger writes are recorded by the orchestrator in the parent audit directory. Source status should not conceal Gu family hold or Bentrup un-emitted ontology/layout claims.
