# Adham’s storage-dam siting profile in Wadi Horan, Iraq

Pete approved five linked Table 1 cards as one regional planning profile. Adham et al. (2018) used these five mapped criteria to identify 39 candidate dam sites. Each evidence card keeps its published classes, labels and scores and points to the other four. PDF pp. 3–4 give the spatial inputs and Table 1. Figure 4 maps suitability on p. 5. Results and candidate sites follow on pp. 6–7.

| Component | Source class → score | Decision for the scoping recipe |
|---|---|---|
| Runoff depth (mm) | <50 → 1, 50–60 → 3, 60–70 → 4, 70–80 → 9, 80–90 → 8 | Keep the reversal and the printed “Medium suitability” label for 80–90. The rainfall period and >90 tail remain unresolved. Soil and land cover enter CN used to estimate runoff. The authors also report correlation between slope and runoff. |
| Slope (%) | <1.5 → 3, 1.5–2.5 → 9, 2.5–4.5 → 5, 4.5–7.5 → 2, >7.5 → 1 | The regional preference peaks at 1.5–2.5%. Keep the separate cited erosion and earthwork caution at ≥5%. |
| Stream order | <4 → 1, 5 → 2, 6 → 3, 7 → 8, >7 → 9 | Figure 2f maps order 4. Both Table 1 and section 2.2.1.5 omit its score. A fourth-order site has unknown rank. |
| Clay content | <8 (other) → 1, 8–11 (sandy clayey loam and sandy loam) → 3, 11–15 (sandy clay) → 4, 15–20 (silty clay) → 7, >20 (clay) → 9 | The study favours retaining water at the storage site. The table and map omit the numeric clay unit. Verify units and class definitions before pooling or binding. |
| Land use/cover | Farmland and grass → 9, moderately cultivated → 7, bare soil → 5, mountain → 1, water body and urban area → restricted | Figure 2d combines water and moist soil. Moderately cultivated land and mountain lack separate map classes. Preserve Table 1 restrictions locally and resolve the crosswalk before scoring. |
