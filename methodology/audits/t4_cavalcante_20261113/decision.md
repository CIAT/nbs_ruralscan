They retained a 5 km analysis radius and used a minimum fragment area of 100 ha. Section 2.2 of the cached preprint (PDF p. 11, lines 217–228) attributes the approach to Crouzeilles et al. (2019).

The distance card previously said that seed-source effects beyond 5 km were negligible. The source describes stronger effects within 5 km and gives no estimate of the effect outside that window.
