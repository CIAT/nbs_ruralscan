## Required corrections and follow-up

1. **Odgaard five indicator anchors have the wrong page.** In `wet_catchment_es_suitability_ecoind_2017/inventory.json`, recreation_potential, biodiversity, nitrogen_mitigation, inverse_land_rent and flash_flood_risk all quote the suitability-direction sentence on **PDF3**, but identify PDF7. PDF7 remains the separately cited weight figure location; split quote-page and weight-page provenance. Gate quote already correctly identifies PDF3.

2. **Watershed compatible-cover availability deserves an explicit T4 candidate referral.** PDF5–6 states: “We also ensured that scenarios were feasible at the RHHU level, meaning that there was enough available and compatible surface area to support the increased wetland cover and contributing area.” Eligible source scenario classes are forest, agriculture, open lands and bare soil. This is actual spatial implementation feasibility in relatively homogeneous hydrological units, not a mere study-site description or hydrological outcome. Preserve as source-specific `land_cover` / system-constraint scoping or model-use candidate, subject to family resolution. `constrained_scenario_only` should describe limited transferability, not imply no T4 information. No universal class ranking, conversion permission, or numeric membership curve follows. Existing-wetland locations/relative abundance allocate the expansion; this is not evidence that only current wetland pixels can be restored. PDF6 explicitly attributes omission of impervious surfaces to high socio-economic removal costs: do not turn this particular rationale into ecological impossibility or a hard legal exclusion. No separate generic suitability threshold derives from 20/50/100/150% expansion levels.

3. **Odgaard organic soils are a useful bounded model-use candidate.** PDF6 identifies potential reconstruction areas from mapped organic soils at 250 m because they “may have earlier been water logged wetlands.” Retain as a historical-wetland proxy candidate with Greve2014 lineage and uncertain transferability. This particular mask is used to extract land-rent data; do not claim it constrains every component of the opportunity map. Canonical-variable mapping remains open: categorical organic-soil mapping is not automatically numeric soil organic carbon. Missing universal calibration is not a reason to discard the proxy.

4. **China targeting model contains more than its final built-up exclusion.** PDF17–18 explicitly uses predicted potential wetlands with current cropland, grassland and woodland to select restoration locations. Keep the categorical overlay as a source-specific system-constraint candidate referral. Woodland conversion tradeoffs are acknowledged on PDF18; inclusion does not authorize conversion. The 30 predictor/importance records are useful **model-use evidence in the upstream potential-distribution component of an actual restoration-targeting workflow**. They must not acquire invented restoration directions, causal claims or memberships. Distinguish occurrence-component role from final implementation overlay; generic variable/family/lineage gates still apply before staging units. A source need not report a directly calibrated restoration response for its model-use evidence to be retained. Regional classifier thresholds remain classifier thresholds, not ecological response cutoffs.

## Spatial prioritization must remain visible

Odgaard's five service-demand/opportunity-cost indicators and three policy weight vectors are genuine geospatial targeting methods. Their separation from structural T4 is correct because they express expected benefits, objectives and costs, not establishment limits. Preserve them as spatial-prioritization method referrals with directions, scenario-specific weights and normalization/scale provenance, rather than summarizing the source as zero useful evidence. PDF12 explicitly calls the result a national/regional screening tool; lack of local feasibility resolution does not invalidate its scoping relevance. No T5/M4/effect extraction is authorized by this review.

The watershed PDF4 upstream isolated/downstream riparian contrast likewise remains a spatial functional-priority referral with Fossey2016 lineage, not a direct establishment curve. Its model's 1% adjacency classification and hydrological budget parameters characterize wetland types and processes; they are not generic siting thresholds.

The semi-arid constructed-wetland source passes because PDF7 relates valley bottoms to runoff/groundwater supply and discusses placement in different catchment zones for landscape diversity. The latter is spatial prioritization, not an increasing-suitability response to valley position. Keep hydrological-placement and diversity-placement referrals distinct. Its treatment-versus-creation family ambiguity remains a real hold; neither a generic valley threshold nor a cross-family treatment-to-creation transfer is justified. The lack of a numeric terrain cutoff is not the reason for that hold.
