# FAO seed-tree proximity: keep the complete conditional recommendation

6 November 2026. Pete explicitly approved: "yes keep the full recommendation including
the 50–100m range and species caveat". Evidence:
`ev_forest_regeneration_potential_fao175_ch4_20261104`.
FAO Forestry Paper 175 (2015), section 4.3, PDF72 / printed50, citing Heidelberg et al.
(2011). Primary source not independently reviewed in this pass.

Retain the full recommendation for dryland assisted natural regeneration:
- Mature, healthy seed trees occur within or near the restoration area.
- Closest seed trees should be no more than **50–100 m** away, **depending on species**.
- Protection/use of existing stump shoots can also support restoration.

The quantified range and its conditions are substantive source evidence for synthesis,
not merely a number to hide behind a qualitative summary. Preserve original modality,
secondary attribution, dryland context and alternative regeneration pathway. The range
concerns individual healthy seed trees, not a distance to a mapped forest edge. Do not
silently substitute the latter or infer that regeneration is impossible beyond a single
universal cutoff. No normalized scores, global exclusion or specific species-to-distance
assignment is invented. Compatible source recommendations can inform later synthesis;
this source alone does not determine the final recipe.
