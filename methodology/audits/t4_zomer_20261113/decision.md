# Zomer’s historical carbon-project screen

Pete approved the six cards together for use as one historical afforestation and reforestation planning profile. Zomer et al. (2008) screened Non-Annex I countries under Kyoto’s Clean Development Mechanism. The profile includes several interventions, including agroforestry and assisted natural regeneration. Enrichment planting was outside the programme scope used by the study. Shared restoration eligibility and scoring remain open.

| Cards | Approved use |
|---|---|
| Aridity | The index divides mean annual precipitation by estimated annual potential evapotranspiration. The exclusion list removes AI <0.65, while the methods and Figure 1 retain AI >0.65. Exactly 0.65 remains unresolved. PDF pp. 2–4. |
| Elevation | The study excluded land above 3,500 m and land at or approaching timberline, using a mean growing-season temperature below 6.5°C as the timberline proxy. The authors assumed slow growth would limit these carbon projects. PDF pp. 2–3. |
| Canopy and baseline | The study used 30% canopy cover on 500 m MODIS VCF pixels to identify forest. The programme called for land that was nonforest at the start of 1990. A 1991 map supplied the historical proxy. Differences in resolution and classification made recent deforestation approximate. PDF pp. 2–3. |
| Land cover, two cards | Count the list across pp. 2–3 once. It comprises water bodies, urban land, tundra, irrigated or otherwise intensively farmed land, current forest and recently cleared forest. Farming exclusions reflected production and food-security concerns. |
| Protected areas | Protected areas were omitted from the main map. A separate assessment found about 27 million ha meeting other criteria inside protected areas. The authors describe restoration in Mt Elgon National Park, Uganda. Figure 2 and PDF pp. 6–7. Legal restrictions for a proposed site require local determination. |

At a protected site that meets the study’s growth criteria, the historical main-map exclusion and the site’s local permission to restore answer different questions. Neither protected status alone nor these historical thresholds establishes an active-planting score. No transfer, shared mask, boundary interpolation, new binding or weight was approved.
