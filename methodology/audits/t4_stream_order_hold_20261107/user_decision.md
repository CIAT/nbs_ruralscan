| Study | Retained siting evidence |
|---|---|
| Kadam et al. (2012), Table 6 | Farm ponds and gully plugs use order 1. Check dams and percolation tanks use orders 1–4 with different slope, runoff, soil and catchment conditions. The authors cite Horton for stream ordering, recommend orders 2–3 for check dams on low-to-moderate slopes, and identify check dams on orders 1–3 in field verification. |
| Singh et al. (2017), Table 6 | Check dams and in-stream percolation tanks use orders 2–3 with different soil and slope conditions. Farm ponds and ground tanks have no stated stream-order condition. The table draws on Jha et al. (2014). |
| Ettazarini (2021), Table 1 | For check dams the table scores orders 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and ≥6 as 0, 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1. The prose calls orders ≥6 unsuitable. The source assigns stream order a criterion weight of 0.10. |
