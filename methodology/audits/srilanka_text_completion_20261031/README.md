## Findings relevant to T4

- **Seasonal habitat visibility (PDF54/61/63):** dry-season salt marsh can look barren;
  seasonal flora may be absent. A bare/low-cover image does not establish restoration
  eligibility. Avicennia presence also does not uniquely distinguish mangrove from salt
  marsh. This qualifies the existing habitat evidence `ev_mg_habitats_sl21_20261029`;
  no seasonal dataset, cutoff or global mask is approved here.
- **Mudflat contradiction remains:** PDF63 explicitly advises against afforesting a
  photographed mudflat. This reinforces the earlier prohibition, but does not resolve
  PDF141 Table9's recommendation to consolidate erosion-prone mudflats and then plant.
  Existing evidence already flags that conflict. New counterevidence stays in this audit;
  no undocumented exception is introduced and no existing EV context is overwritten.
- **Species occurrence is not a planting whitelist:** Table3 lists associates, including
  Annona glabra, while Table5 identifies it as invasive. These are different purposes,
  not evidence that the guide endorses planting it. PDF41/43/67 preserve locally observed
  species composition and Nypa-associated zones, without converting regional occurrence
  into universal climatic tolerance or excluding the whole restoration family elsewhere.
- **Hydrological direction needs context:** PDF48 warns against altering undisturbed
  sedimentation and water movement. PDF78–79 describe freshwater diversions and saltwater
  restriction as threats. These do not support a globally increasing freshwater-supply
  response; neither sediment supply nor freshwater flow can be mapped to unrelated VONT
  outcomes/proxies. No quantitative salinity, redox or flow calibration recovered.
- **Soil-pH intention clarified, form defect retained:** PDF91 requests pH of soil and
  water; PDF93 explicitly describes a soil-pH meter. Thus soil pH is intended, but PDF171
  still places its only pH column under Water. The previous annex audit's missing-column
  concern remains valid. This is measurement intent, not suitability-model use or a curve.
- **Protected areas:** the 2021 guide requires agency approval for restoration within
  protected areas (PDF28). It does not establish a blanket exclusion. This audit records
  source wording only; it does not verify present law or create a legal mask.
