# Legacy Word supplement intake

For the publisher-hosted `.doc` supplements referenced by Pinto 2019, use the same immutable-render principle as the existing `.docx` adapter: acquire from the publisher link attached to the DOI-verified parent article; retain original `.doc` and its checksum; render once with LibreOffice into a uniquely named PDF; preserve PDF checksum, conversion version and parent DOI in the intake manifest. All quotes use one-based PDF-page locators against that saved render. Inspect every rendered page before extraction; validate quote and number provenance centrally. No raw `.doc` goes into `.cache/corpus` (PDF only). This rule does not authorise ingestion of spreadsheets or other dataset formats. Supplemental evidence cannot count as an independent paper from its parent.
