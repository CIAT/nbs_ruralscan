Hierarchy: **agroforestry → suitability family → variable/rule**. Practices are members of a family, not a second parallel classification. Show practice restrictions only for subset rules (windbreaks/shelterbelts). Coffee/cocoa/tea are crop parameters. Shared evidence does not automatically apply across families.

## Rebuild safely

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/synthesise-recipe.py agroforestry
python3 src/nbs_ruralscan/schema_tools/generate.py schema
python3 src/nbs_ruralscan/schema_tools/structure.py schema
```

Publication defaults to **approved-only**; `--approved-only` remains an explicit alias. `--include-unreviewed` requires isolated `--output-dir` or `--dry-run` and cannot write the live recipe. Numeric synthesis preserves authored categorical gates and design rows. Regression tests compare regenerated records against live T4.

`T4_agroforestry__<family>.json` files are numeric synthesis intermediates, not replacements for the assembled table: authored categorical gates are retained in `T4_suitability_mappings`. Reports include separate crop lanes and dispositions. Fix evidence encodings in EV and regenerate; do not hand-edit generated JSON or infer missing bounds.

## Dataset bindings and execution limits

Use shared [T1](../../T1_data_registry.csv), [BIND](../../registers/BIND_dataset_binding.csv), [VONT](../../registers/VONT_variable_ontology.csv), [FAM](../../registers/FAM_family_registry.csv), [EV](../../registers/EV_evidence_register.csv) and [SRC](../../registers/SRC_source_register.csv). `runtime/host_inputs.py` resolves host inputs; `runtime/categorical.py` evaluates gates from aligned caller-supplied arrays. Neither is a complete raster acquisition or M1 runner.

- MapSPAM 2020 physical crop areas supply global crop-host fractions; better observed commodity maps may override. Model probabilities are not area fractions.
- Boundary geometry, regional placement width, local grazing/forage confirmation and farmland identity remain explicit input requirements. No GPW+GLW replacement for local confirmation has been approved.
- Missing codes/masks remain unknown. Preserve native support and crop fractions; do not count whole pixels as crop footprint or linear planting area.
- Eight soft operational scenario rows belong to M2b / implementation guidance, outside structural suitability.
- Retained protected-area row has text threshold `IUCN_Cat_I_II` and planted-silvoarable scope. It needs explicit legal category-mask handling; categorical host execution alone does not apply it across all families. Resolve legal-mask coverage before claiming complete opportunity-space output.

### Source-review resolution and variable importance (20 October)

All 44 active unresolved structural records were checked against full source context:
37 corrected/verified and seven soft-quarantined for wrong-table or wrong-variable claims.
These decisions are explicitly stamped **Codex source verification**, not fabricated human approvals.
Contradictory source classes and missing dataset crosswalks remain visible as compilation holds.
Nath pH, rainfall and soil carbon now compile as explicit regional profiles. Soil carbon below
0.6% is marginal, not unsuitable; missing pH ranges were present in the full table.

`final_audit/verified_model_use.csv` and `variable_use_summary.csv` under `pipeline/t4_audit/agroforestry/`
report explicit source-verified model use independently of threshold validity. One paper gets
one vote per variable/scope (DOI deduplication). This verification subset is incomplete;
legacy `CV_lit_review_agroforestry.csv` counts are candidate evidence, not confirmed model-use counts.
Missing verified use is not evidence of non-use. Crop/species models remain separate.

Three different weights must not be confused:
- Response synthesis: source tier × claim basis × grey-literature adjustment.
- Source importance: reported model-specific AHP/expert weights, RF weights or permutation importance;
  retain source, model, page, scale and nested denominator. Never pool unlike metrics directly.
- Current T4 defaults: equal weights; not claimed to be evidence-derived importance.

Nath topsoil carbon's single branch is 25% × 20% × 20% = 1% of the final model.
Its topsoil pH additionally occurs in the rooting branch; the reported branch weight is not
its total. Saraheb's environmental priority weights of 5 normalize by 17 after halving
water-quality weights, not by 19 or 100; these are not structural suitability weights.

FMNR rainfall remains a candidate with observed-context evidence. The old 300–800 mm range
and 400–800 mm optimum were not defensible establishment limits. WRI explicitly permits
regreening below 400 mm; greatest observed spread does not define an upper exclusion.
