## Reading the comparison

- **WOCAT ratings now sit in every cell** (538 effect + 126 asset-vulnerability + 26 operational units, grey,
  `expert_assertion` / `practitioner_rating`). They cannot raise `evidence_level` on their own (`_LOW_BASIS`), take the
  grey discount, and pull strength classes toward their central +1/+2 ratings: flood livelihood mitigation moved
  `very_high → moderate` (literature's 50 % runoff cuts vs compilers' "moderately decreased"), fire gained a
  `moderate` class from three sheets, wind/cyclone appeared (`low`), and waterlogging turned a `negative` cell into
  one with 18 sources (compilers rate drainage improved; Zougmoré's wet-year waterlogging stays in the pool).
- **Asset threats** now cover drought, flood, heat, fire, wind, frost + the asset-only `sedimentation` /
  `extreme_rainfall` (compilers' QT 6.3 coping); `asset_risk_weight` stays blank until waterlogging has an asset row.
- **Drought** stays `very_high`: the direct drought-year contrasts set the strength, 69 sources (38 peer-reviewed units)
  carry the confidence; the four null units remain in the agreement note.
- **Seeds vs generated** now agree on drought and erosion; seeds over-stated water_stress confidence and mis-signed
  waterlogging; farming-system cells still have no system-specific evidence (every seed system cell → the all-systems row).
- **Economics** are ordinal and denominator-specific; WOCAT cost tables (QT 4.x) are not yet in the adapter —
  a `cost_calculation` / `establishment_cost_breakdown` pass is the obvious round-3 item.
- **Bias pattern:** independent evaluations most sceptical; Bank completion reports and compiler self-ratings most
  positive — both discounted, both visible in `source_mix`.
