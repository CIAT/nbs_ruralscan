## Reading the comparison

- **The drought wall is `low` by rule, not by evidence absence.** 31 units feed the drought roll-up, but nearly all are
  `direction_only` (narrative statements) — the large drought-year yield results (zaï 300–400 kg/ha vs 0 in the 1990
  Niger drought; stone bunds 2–3× yield in dry years) were extracted as `crop_specific` + `taxon` and are therefore routed
  OUT of the practice-level cell by the species/crop lock. Whether WH crop-specific yields should be re-includable
  (`allow_crop_scope`, as for agroforestry F5) is a Pete decision — see `methodology/effect_evidence_followups.md` §1e.
- **Seeds were optimistic.** Every seed T3 drought cell was `high`/`very_high` with `high` confidence; the generated
  cells carry very_high confidence (robust × high agreement on direction) but `low` strength. The seeds' agro-pastoral and
  pastoral drought cells have no system-specific evidence; the `pastoral_rangeland` row is the all-systems value.
- **Negative evidence now shows.** `waterlogging` is `negative` (stone bunds cause waterlogging in wet years —
  Zougmoré); seed said `moderate` mitigation. Diversion banquettes raising erosion/peak flows (Heusch, Roose) sit in the
  terracing family rows.
- **Fire** rests on one Roose 2006 narrative unit — effectively no evidence, as the seed (`none`) said.
- **Economics** are now ordinal and denominator-specific (treatment cost per ha of SWC works from IEG/ICRs; cost per
  cistern/structure in BRL; labour days per ha) — the seed's single `establishment_cost = moderate_negative` is replaced by
  cost-class rows; 4 units excluded (income band ≠ row band, no denominator).
- **Bias pattern (from the lane reports):** independent evaluations (IEG, IFPRI, SPIA, MCC, DIME) are the most sceptical;
  Bank completion reports are the most positive and are tiered `low`; Embrapa self-assessments tiered `low`.
