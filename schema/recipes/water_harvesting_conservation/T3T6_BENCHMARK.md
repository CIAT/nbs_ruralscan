## Reading the comparison

- **The drought wall was a routing + encoding gap, not crop scope.** `crop_yield` / income / food security had no T3
  route; drought-year yield contrasts ("two to three times", "only fields with pits produced a harvest", 0 → 300–400
  kg/ha) carried `strength_class = unspecified`. With the 37 livelihood-outcome routes (stated hazard required), the
  v1.6.2 `contrast` / `complete_contrast` / `fold_change` encodings and the strength median taken over the modal-sign
  units only (nulls lower agreement, not strength), drought and flood read `very_high` at very_high confidence. The four
  null drought units (Bidi driest-year no-gain, sand-dam capacity, two ns programme results) are visible in the
  agreement note and the prose conditionality.
- **Seeds vs generated now mostly agree on drought/flood/erosion**; seeds were optimistic on water_stress only in
  confidence, and wrong on waterlogging (`negative`: stone bunds cause waterlogging in wet years — Zougmoré) and fire
  (one narrative unit). Farming-system cells have no system-specific evidence, so every seed system cell maps to the
  all-systems row.
- **Asset threats** now include `sedimentation` (6 sources: dams, tanks and check dams silting within years) and
  `extreme_rainfall` (storm damage to terraces) as asset-only hazards; `asset_risk_weight` stays blank (7-hazard rule).
- **Economics** are ordinal and denominator-specific (per ha treated · per structure · per m³ of masonry — see the
  `usd_per_m3` semantics note in followups §1e); the seed's single `establishment_cost = moderate_negative` is replaced by
  cost-class rows.
- **Bias pattern:** independent evaluations (IEG, IFPRI, SPIA, MCC, DIME) are the most sceptical; Bank completion
  reports the most positive (tiered `low`, bundled results at half weight); Embrapa self-assessments `low`.
