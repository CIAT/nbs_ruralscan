How to read Table 6.1

  1. Footprint is room, not a target. It is where the practice could go, before anyone asks a farmer.
  2. Overlap is not compatibility. Two practices can share a cell on the map and still compete for the same field.
  3. Strength and confidence are separate. A strong benefit seen in few studies is still low confidence (full profiles in Section 6.2).
  4. Affordable area is an order of magnitude. It tells you whether the budget buys a pilot or a programme, not what a project would cost (Section 6.3).

Costs counted

Cost ranges are recorded in US dollars. Affordable area is shown only for a US dollar budget; the scan does not convert currencies.

Assumptions behind the affordable area

  1. Affordable area = budget ÷ cost per unit, from the highest to the lowest cost in the range.
  2. “With 3 years of maintenance” adds three years of the maintenance cost per unit.
  3. Costs are in US dollars from mixed sources and years, undiscounted; no currency conversion.
  4. Labour and inputs are inside the cost ranges, not added again.
  5. No delivery, monitoring or overhead costs are included.
