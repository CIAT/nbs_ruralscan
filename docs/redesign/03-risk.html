3.2How to read Figure 3.1

  1. Risk to the investment, not to people. This screen asks what could damage the planting. Risk to rural livelihoods is a need layer in Stage 04. The two are kept apart and never added together.
  2. Young trees are the exposed asset. Exposure is judged over the establishment period — five years here — when a fire or a flood can undo the planting.
  3. A flag is exposure, not a loss rate. It says the hazard is common here, not how much would be lost. There is no overall risk score.
  4. Two separate questions. What a practice can reduce for farmers and what can damage the practice itself are answered independently. A practice can ease flooding downstream and still be washed out as seedlings.

3.4.3Why there is no overall risk ratingFire and flood do different damage and call for different responses.

Adding fire and flood into one score would hide which hazard is driving a flag, and so which response fits — firebreaks and timing for fire, site and species choice for flood. Each hazard keeps its own flag, and the “Both” view shows where they coincide.
