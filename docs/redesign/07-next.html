Scoping brief — planted silvoarable agroforestry, Sierra Leone (example)

Agroforestry, planted silvoarable · classes Very high + High · Sierra Leone · 5 km cells · baseline 1995–2014 · budget USD 5,000,000 (2026) · example data

Assumptions

  1. Cell areas are nominal: each 5 km cell counts as 2,500 ha.
  2. Suitability rules are the practice’s published envelope, unadjusted locally.
  3. Legal exclusions use global boundary datasets.
  4. Need layers are 5 km proxies chosen for the stated priorities.
  5. Hazard flags cover a five-year establishment period.
  6. Costs are establishment ranges from mixed sources, undiscounted.

Questions for feasibility

  1. What do seedlings, labour and land cost locally?
  2. Who holds tenure, and how will free, prior and informed consent be sought?
  3. Which parcels are excluded at field scale?
  4. How does planting affect local hydrology and water use?
  5. Will farmers adopt, and on what terms?
  6. What baseline and comparison design will monitoring use?

7.2Before you use the brief

Scoping gives the shape of the problem and the opportunity. Implementing without bottom-up refinement with partners carries a high risk of poor return.

Reuse as is

  • Maps and district summaries
  • Headline figures, with their caveats
  • The assumptions list

Redo locally

  • Costs and prices
  • Exclusions at parcel scale
  • Need, with district or village data
  • Who takes part, and how
