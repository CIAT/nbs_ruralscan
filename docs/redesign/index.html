Atlas: one question, one figure, per stage

All seven stages in direction A, where each stage is a chapter — a question, one dominant map or table, a short reading column, and optional depth — plus a grey-box sketch of the workbench alternative for comparison. All maps draw a synthetic example surface on real Sierra Leone boundaries; no thresholds, weights, evidence or schema tables are used.
