T4: evidence by agroforestry system

Read across to compare systems. Counts stay within assigned system; shared regional evidence is not automatically inherited. “System not assigned” means source applicability still needs a system/context decision. No percentage support shown: this is an evidence inventory, not a complete screened-paper denominator. Counts deduplicate DOI/source identity; secondary-source lineage still requires checking before independent agreement is claimed.

Evidence papers count active general-practice structural records; human-reviewed means at least one retained record for that paper and cell was approved, not that every claim is settled. Verified model use is separate source-checked metadata. Direction counts require explicit encoding and do not create numeric curves. Dropped, crop/species and soft scenario evidence stay outside these general-practice counts. Configured crop-host rules remain visible independently.

Boundary plantings require two distinct inputs: land cover describes the host land use; field-boundary zone identifies placement geometry and a locally justified buffer. Boundary geometry is not a land-cover class. Other required inputs are likewise shown separately, without duplicating evidence votes.

Configured does not mean ready everywhere: piecewise rules can require explicit regional profile selection; categorical rules require their dataset inputs. No new weight or applicability approval follows from this matrix.
