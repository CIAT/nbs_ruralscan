Agroforestry: what harmonization changes

15 source profiles · 55 class bands · 18 scores changed · 1 unresolved score band · 0 approved combined pools

Scores now follow the approved common class convention. Below, “previous score” means the previous compiler’s rank ÷ full-scale maximum—not necessarily a score reported by the paper. Source ranks and cutoffs remain traceable. Slope conversions now retain full precision.

What still needs review?
  • System and context applicability: no compatible cross-paper pools have yet been assigned in schema. Most multi-paper records are regional, unassigned agroforestry evidence. Their cutoffs are comparable for inspection, but not yet authorized as one universal system response.
  • Favourable rainfall above 1,200 mm: Mushtaq’s retained statement does not identify an ordinal class or calibrated score. The range remains recorded, but its previous score of 1 is now unknown.
  • FMNR rainfall: observed study ranges and spread remain evidence, not an establishment response curve. Harmonization cannot turn those descriptions into cutoffs.
  • Class meanings: marginal→low, good→high and very good→very high use the approved semantic convention. They remain design crosswalks, not measured calibration.

Engine can combine explicitly approved compatible pools. No automatic pool was invented for these records. Ties and conflicting exclusion claims stay unknown when evaluated.
