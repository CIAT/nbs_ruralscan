B2 · Boundary geometry and buffer convention

Linear / boundary — windbreaks and shelterbelts · field_boundary_zone

Current behaviour

Cropland and pasture branches remain unknown without a supplied boundary zone. Grassland also needs managed_pasture_present.

What this input means

A zone beside real field boundaries where windbreak placement can be scoped. A classified grassland/cropland pixel is not a field boundary.

Source evidence and limits

Castle, Sarah; Miller, Daniel C.; Wardropper, Chloe B. (2025). Mapping the social-ecological suitability of agroforestry in the US Midwest. Environmental Research Letters. · page 8
ev_t4_land_cover_castle2025_windbreak

Windbreaks: 10 m buffers around delineated fields USDA Crop Sequence Boundaries [67] 10 m buffers around delineated fields and on areas currently in cropland or pasture/grassland use.

Evidence limit: Castle uses 10 m buffers in US Midwest. Pete approved variable regional width, not a universal 10 m. Contour buffers remain outside this gate.

Dataset options

  • USDA Crop Sequence Boundaries — Estimated field polygons for contiguous US; reference implementation, not an Africa-wide input.
  • Local parcel or validated field-delineation layer — Candidate only: pilot coverage, rights, age and accuracy still to establish.

Recommendation — not applied

Keep boundary geometry required; treat width as regional configuration. Agent searches coverage once pilot AOI known.

Proposed rule

Use validated field polygons/lines, buffer in projected metres using a documented width and convention (total width or width on each side). Remove duplicate shared edges. Report unique line length; buffered zone is applicability, not planted area. No numeric width selected here.

Missing-data fallback

No geometry: unknown for this family, with a gap flag. Do not invent boundaries from coarse land-cover pixels.

Who does the work

Agent: source search, geometry QA, implementation. Pete: policy/region-specific width if known.

Information needed from you

Pilot country/AOI; known boundary source if any. Optional width evidence, metres, and total-versus-each-side convention.

Decision

Accept geometry-first approach, with width deferred to regional evidence and no global default?

G1 · Grazing: measured condition or scoping proxy?

Silvopastoral · grazing_system_present

Current behaviour

Only WorldCover grassland (30) enters this branch; missing grazing mask makes it unknown.

What this input means

Grassland used by grazing livestock; not merely livestock somewhere in a coarse grid cell.

Source evidence and limits

Castle, Sarah; Miller, Daniel C.; Wardropper, Chloe B. (2025). Mapping the social-ecological suitability of agroforestry in the US Midwest. Environmental Research Letters. · page 8
ev_t4_land_cover_castle2025_2

Areas currently in pasture/grassland use. Silvopasture includes growing any arrangement of trees and forage with grazing animals.

Evidence limit: Castle requires grazing animals but supplies no globally transferable livestock-density cutoff. Pete suggested GPW and GLW4; this card asks about the actual proxy rule.

Dataset options

  • GPW dominant grassland class — 30 m, cultivated and natural/semi-natural classes; no direct grazing measurement.
  • FAO GLW4 — 2020 modelled livestock distribution; housed animals and mobile herds limit inference.

Recommendation — not applied

Allow a separately flagged scoping-proxy scenario; retain locally confirmed management as preferred input.

Proposed rule

Candidate scenario: grassland (GPW class 1 OR 2) AND any positive cattle/buffalo/goat/sheep density in GLW4. >0 means modelled presence, not a validated suitability cutoff. Keep native coarse livestock support visible. Missing either input remains unknown. Test extent and sensitivity before adopting as default. GPW versus current WorldCover extent must be compared explicitly.

Missing-data fallback

Without proxy approval or local confirmation: retain unknown. Zero density must not prove non-grazing when model coverage is uncertain.

Who does the work

Agent: construct and compare masks, report uncertainty. Pete: accept or reject proxy policy.

Information needed from you

Pilot AOI/year; known livestock-management survey if available. No personal dataset upload required to approve a trial.

Decision

Authorize GPW grassland + positive GLW4 ruminants as a flagged trial scenario, not a confirmed default?

G2 · Pasture management for boundary planting

Linear / boundary — grassland branch only · managed_pasture_present

Current behaviour

Cropland boundary branch does not need this mask; grassland boundary branch does.

What this input means

Pasture managed as part of an agricultural host system, including managed semi-natural grassland; not all natural grassland is pasture.

Source evidence and limits

Castle, Sarah; Miller, Daniel C.; Wardropper, Chloe B. (2025). Mapping the social-ecological suitability of agroforestry in the US Midwest. Environmental Research Letters. · page 8
ev_t4_land_cover_castle2025_windbreak

Windbreaks: 10 m buffers around delineated fields USDA Crop Sequence Boundaries [67] 10 m buffers around delineated fields and on areas currently in cropland or pasture/grassland use.

Evidence limit: Source says pasture/grassland. Additional management requirement is a conservative crosswalk choice; source does not prescribe a global managed-pasture classifier.

Dataset options

  • GPW + GLW4 — Same supporting layers as G1; cultivated grassland may be ungrazed forage.
  • Local pasture/land-use inventory — Candidate, not acquired.

Recommendation — not applied

Reuse approved G1 proxy policy for a trial, avoiding a second independently invented classifier.

Proposed rule

If G1 trial approved: reuse its grassland/grazing candidate for this branch, then require field-boundary geometry. GPW cultivated class alone is insufficient. Confirmed pasture can override proxy. G2 cannot authorize G1 implicitly.

Missing-data fallback

If no local confirmation and G1 not approved: grassland branch remains unknown; cropland boundary branch can still run.

Who does the work

Agent: reuse mask and report inherited limitations. Pete: decide acceptable transfer.

Information needed from you

No extra upload if G1 trial selected; local pasture inventory optional.

Decision

Reuse G1 flagged proxy for boundary grassland branch, conditional on separate G1 approval?

F1 · Ungrazed forage: limited silvoarable branch

Planted silvoarable — grassland branch only · managed_forage_without_grazing

Current behaviour

WorldCover cropland (40) already passes host gate; only grassland (30) needs this mask.

What this input means

Forage grown between planted tree rows without grazing animals: e.g. cut-and-carry or harvested fodder.

Source evidence and limits

Castle, Sarah; Miller, Daniel C.; Wardropper, Chloe B. (2025). Mapping the social-ecological suitability of agroforestry in the US Midwest. Environmental Research Letters. · page 8
ev_t4_land_cover_castle2025_1

Areas currently in cropland or pasture/grassland use. Excluding lands that are currently planted with tree crops. Alley cropping on pasture/grasslands would consist of growing forage between the tree rows without grazing animals.

Evidence limit: Castle explicitly distinguishes ungrazed forage from silvopasture. GPW cannot establish absence of grazing; zero modelled livestock is not proof.

Dataset options

  • Local forage-use or farm-management map — Preferred, but not acquired.
  • GPW — Can locate candidate managed grassland, not confirm ungrazed use.

Recommendation — not applied

Keep this optional branch local-data dependent; do not block ordinary cropland silvoarable execution.

Proposed rule

Pass grassland only where a documented management input confirms forage without grazing. No automatic GPW/GLW4 absence inference.

Missing-data fallback

Unknown for unsupported grassland branch; cropland continues. Grazed systems route to silvopastoral.

Who does the work

Agent: retain branch-specific gap flag. Pete: confirm acceptable scope.

Information needed from you

Only supply management data if this branch matters in pilot; otherwise no action needed.

Decision

Keep ungrazed-forage branch conditional on local confirmation, while proceeding on cropland?

D1 · FMNR on cropland: is depletion a prerequisite?

Regeneration farmland — cropland branch · depleted_farmland

Current behaviour

Cropland cannot pass current gate without depletion mask.

What this input means

Source describes improving depleted farmland; no operational definition of depletion is encoded.

Source evidence and limits

WOCAT (2019). Farmer Managed Natural Regeneration (FMNR). WOCAT SLM Technologies Database, Technology 507 (Kenya). Compiler: T. Kalytta; ed. I. Ojuok et al. · page 1
EV_wocat_507_2019_008

Farmer Managed Natural Regeneration (FMNR) is a proven SLM Technology to restore degraded wasteland and improve depleted farmland.

Evidence limit: Single Kenyan WOCAT applicability statement; no threshold, time window, classifier or claim that non-depleted cropland is unsuitable. Hard gate is stronger than quoted evidence.

Dataset options

  • Local soil/farm condition assessment — Could characterize depletion if definition and sampling support are supplied.
  • UNCCD land-degradation guidance — Uses land cover, productivity and soil organic carbon; regional degradation does not directly define farm nutrient depletion.

Recommendation — not applied

Remove depletion as a universal hard eligibility requirement; retain it as restoration-targeting context. This is a proposed T4 change, not yet applied.

Proposed rule

Allow existing cropland as host context; attach depleted/unknown condition as descriptor where available. Do not infer actual FMNR establishment success. Rootstock and enabling conditions stay in their agreed operational/M6 route. Do not substitute low SOC or low NDVI as an unvalidated cutoff.

Missing-data fallback

Until decision: current mask remains unknown. If proposed change approved: missing depletion data would no longer exclude/withhold cropland host eligibility.

Who does the work

Agent: revise schema only after decision; identify context indicators. Pete: methodological gate-versus-context decision.

Information needed from you

No raster required to answer this policy question. If retaining gate, specify indicator, reference period and defensible cutoff/source.

Decision

Remove universal depleted_farmland gate and retain depletion as restoration-targeting context?

D2 · FMNR outside cropland: degradation versus farmland identity

Regeneration farmland — grass/shrub/bare branches · degraded_farmland

Current behaviour

WorldCover grassland (30), shrubland (20) and bare/sparse (60) require degraded_farmland.

What this input means

Land independently identified as agricultural/fallow/former farmland suitable for the family context, with degradation separately characterized. Bare/shrub/grass cover alone cannot establish this.

Source evidence and limits

WOCAT (2019). Farmer Managed Natural Regeneration (FMNR). WOCAT SLM Technologies Database, Technology 507 (Kenya). Compiler: T. Kalytta; ed. I. Ojuok et al. · page 1
EV_wocat_507_2019_008

Farmer Managed Natural Regeneration (FMNR) is a proven SLM Technology to restore degraded wasteland and improve depleted farmland.

Evidence limit: WOCAT describes degraded wasteland but gives no class-to-farmland crosswalk or universal degradation threshold. Natural grasslands/shrublands must not become restoration targets by default.

Dataset options

  • Local land-use history / restoration plan — Preferred for farmland identity; not acquired.
  • UNCCD land-degradation guidance — Possible condition descriptor; not evidence of farmland identity or FMNR suitability.

Recommendation — not applied

Replace ambiguous degradation gate with explicit farmland-context requirement; treat degradation as targeting context. Keep these branches unknown until farmland identity can be established.

Proposed rule

For grass/shrub/bare classes, require validated agricultural/fallow/former-farmland context. Degradation flag may characterize restoration need but cannot establish host identity. No automatic opening of natural ecosystems. Rootstock remains separately routed.

Missing-data fallback

No farmland context: unknown, even if degradation product flags the pixel. Cropland branch follows separate D1 decision.

Who does the work

Agent: locate land-use history inputs; implement separated condition after approval. Pete: confirm scope safeguard.

Information needed from you

No immediate upload required; pilot AOI needed for source search. If known, identify local fallow/farmland or restoration inventory.

Decision

Separate farmland identity from degradation, retaining a hard context check for non-cropland branches?

C1 · Crop hosts: approved fallback, optional local refinement

Shaded perennial · coffee_host_fraction | cocoa_host_fraction | tea_host_fraction

Current behaviour

OR crop gates and MapSPAM fractions already approved. Sources/bands configured; no AOI processed.

What this input means

Fraction of pixel occupied by supported crop; host extent, not shade benefit, shade deficit or suitability strength.

Source evidence and limits

Jezeer et al. (2017). Shaded Coffee and Cocoa – Double Dividend for Biodiversity and Small-scale Farmers. Ecological Economics. · page 4
ev_economic_return_jezeer17_1

Additionally, the average net return of shaded systems in the subset was signiﬁcantly higher by 23% (p b 0.05; t = 2.31; df = 14), showing a higher proﬁt per hectare for farmers with shade trees planted between their coffee and cocoa plants.

Evidence limit: Jezeer supports coffee/cocoa shaded-system identity. Tea is Pete-approved design scope, not evidence from that paper. Other crops need explicit configured scope.

Dataset options

  • MapSPAM 2020 v2r2 — Physical area ha: Arabica + Robusta, cocoa, tea; divide by native pixel area.
  • Accurate local commodity map — Preferred where independently validated; not yet selected.

Recommendation — not applied

Proceed with already-approved MapSPAM fallback once AOI chosen. Treat local map request as optional refinement, not a new approval blocker.

Proposed rule

OR across positive crop fractions; preserve crop identities and fractions. Unknown components remain unknown where no other crop passes. Do not add duplicate technology totals or count whole coarse pixels as crop area. Local overrides need compatible units/provenance.

Missing-data fallback

MapSPAM remains usable if no better map is supplied. Existing SLE upload preference does not block fallback.

Who does the work

Agent: source acquisition, normalization and QA. Pete: AOI and optional local source knowledge.

Information needed from you

Pilot country/AOI. Optional commodity-map link, year, crop classes and known limitations.

Decision

Any known local commodity map to assess, or proceed with approved fallback?
