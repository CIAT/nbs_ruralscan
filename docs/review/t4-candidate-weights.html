What evidence agrees?

In this bounded three-paper panel, slope and annual rainfall each appear in 3/3 models. Source tables favour lower slope (3/3) and higher annual rainfall within reported contexts (3/3). These are source-inspection counts, not new human approvals or full-corpus support percentages. Direction agreement does not prove matching cutoffs. Suitability calibration holds remain separate.

Mean annual temperature appears in Singh and Nath (2/3), both increasing within study context; Haile does not include it. It is retained in the source matrix but omitted from this common-input weight experiment. Soil pH, organic carbon and texture cannot yet be pooled across unspecified versus topsoil/subsoil depth definitions. Wetness, drainage and water proximity measure different things. Access inputs stay outside structural T4; they remain in each original model's importance denominator.

Haile: what can be resolved?

Section 3.2 explicitly describes Table 5 as AHP-derived weights. Recomputing its printed matrix by column normalisation and row means yields 34.55, 29.54, 18.95, 10.48 and 6.48%, closely matching printed 34.5, 29.5, 18.9, 10.5 and 6.5%.
