Confirmed losses: Chuma’s numeric slope payload classified as qualitative; Haile’s soil-carbon table continuation omitted; Ahmad2020’s image-based methods table under-extracted. Missing registration does not mean every paper supplies establishment cutoffs.

Castle’s agroforestry GEE code: host masks and placement rules, nine species-growth layers, plus environmental priorities and feasibility. Paper and code share one evidence lineage.
