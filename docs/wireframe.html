Background before settings

Scoping nature-based solutions for rural investment

Nature-based solutions protect, restore or manage ecosystems to address challenges such as climate risk, degraded land and insecure livelihoods. Rural NbS Scan helps teams explore where these approaches could fit in agricultural, forestry and other rural landscapes.

For World Bank Task Team Leaders, country partners and technical teams preparing early investment concepts.

Why this project?

Investment teams need to connect landscape conditions with development priorities before choosing interventions and places for closer study.

This World Bank-funded project, led by the Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT, develops a methodology toolkit, pilot applications in two least developed countries and reproducible analysis notebooks.

What is the scoping tool for?

Connect where practices could fit with the people, livelihoods and ecosystems in those areas. Compare opportunities against priorities such as climate resilience, poverty, biodiversity and equity.

The output is a shortlist for further investigation, with risks, assumptions and evidence gaps—not an approved investment.

Why this wireframe?

Explore the method's questions, choices and proposed outputs, and tell us where they fail to support your decisions. This is an interactive sketch, not the final UI design.

The project implements its method through reproducible analyses. A production dashboard is not a contracted deliverable.

How does a 100-ha boundary relate to coarse cells?

A 100-ha square is 1 km × 1 km: 4% of a 5 km × 5 km cell. A vector boundary can be displayed even when inputs cannot resolve conditions inside it.

A small exclusion can disappear under coarse rasterisation. Clipping, fractional coverage and exclusion rules must be specified by the runtime; displaying the polygon does not prove a correct analytical mask.

Use finer, fit-for-purpose local inputs and site assessment. Terrain derivatives must be derived at a meaningful scale before aggregation.

Baseline, future and difference: how to read them

Baseline stands for 1995–2014 and future for SSP2-4.5 in 2041–2060. Values are pre-authored examples, not model outputs. Difference shows class changes; summaries compare both footprints. Practice, geography and chosen classes stay fixed.

These are climate-context comparisons. They cannot estimate benefits of investing now, avoided losses or the cost of delay: that requires an intervention counterfactual.

2. Is this NbS vulnerable, and what evidence is needed?

Exposure is only the first question. Establishment stage, species or structure, hazard intensity, duration, design and management influence consequences. The synthetic fire and flood masks do not supply those answers.

Current evidence status: vulnerability of this proposed investment is unassessed. The method requires asset-threat evidence; livelihood-mitigation evidence cannot be inverted to produce it.

2. Co-produce the local next steps
  • Discuss priorities with local institutions, communities and rights holders, including women and IPLCs.
  • Check included, excluded and unassessed areas against local data, legal permissions and practical access.
  • Assess species/practice design, establishment vulnerability, labour, tenure, markets and adoption incentives.
  • Commission proportional feasibility, water balance, safeguards and cost studies. Agree baselines, unique beneficiaries and monitoring before claiming results.

Implementation and review notes
Presentation revision implements worked examples, not analytical integration. The frozen original remains v0.
Decisions and limits
  • Mode A-only walkthrough is Pete's presentation choice. Method Mode B is retained; no schema or analytical default changed.
  • Nominal area, people, hazard flags, suitability and priority classes come from the synthetic fixture. They have no evidentiary standing.
  • Admin 2 geography comes from the basemap; Admin 3 quadrants are deliberately schematic. No invented official boundary dataset.
  • Two objective scenarios are pre-authored; custom objectives have no ranking. There is no hidden MCDA or live AI.
  • Dataset proposals remain unreviewed. No documents are uploaded, parsed or sent to an AI service.
  • Saved settings and results identify fixture version, all controls and assumptions. Reopening an example is not executing the pipeline.
  • T4–Diego alignment, authoritative asset-vulnerability evidence, subnational gender data and production exports remain pending.
